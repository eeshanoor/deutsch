SIEBEN TAGE DEUTSCH - iPhone app setup

The app has to be online once (free hosting), then it installs on your iPhone and works offline.

1. PUT IT ONLINE (on your PC, about 5 minutes, free)
   a. Go to github.com and sign in (or create a free account).
   b. Click "New" repository. Name it: deutsch. Choose Public. Click "Create repository".
   c. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in all the files from this folder
      (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, the three icon PNGs). Click "Commit changes".
   d. Open Settings -> Pages. Under "Branch" pick "main" and "/ (root)", then Save.
   e. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is at: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/deutsch/

2. INSTALL ON IPHONE
   a. Open that link in Safari (it must be Safari).
   b. Tap the Share button -> "Add to Home Screen" -> Add.
   c. Open it from the new "Deutsch" icon once while online. After that it works offline.

3. FIRST-TIME IPHONE SETTINGS
   - Microphone: tap Allow when the app asks.
   - Speech recognition: Settings -> Siri -> turn on Siri, and
     Settings -> General -> Keyboard -> Enable Dictation.
   - German voice: Settings -> Accessibility -> Spoken Content -> Voices -> German -> download Anna.
   - If the mic still won't work, the app switches to dictation: tap the keyboard's mic key and speak.

Your progress is saved on the phone. Deleting the home-screen icon deletes it.
